Our Purpose

Democratizing the Universe

Space belongs to everyone. Our mission is to break down complex astrophysics and make the beauty of the cosmos accessible to anyone with a screen.

Accessible Education

We strip away the confusing jargon. From quantum mechanics to backyard stargazing, we provide free, easy-to-understand resources for all ages.

Live Cosmic Data

Powered by real-time APIs from NASA and ESA, we bring live satellite tracking, interactive sky maps, and JWST discoveries directly to your fingertips.

Global Community

Whether you're looking up from a city balcony in Thrissur or a remote dark sky reserve, we connect space enthusiasts globally to share knowledge and wonder.

Our History

How We Started Looking Up

What began as a late-night hobby in a backyard has evolved into a global platform for space exploration.

August 2020

The First Telescope

It all started with a simple 70mm refractor telescope and a clear night sky. Seeing the rings of Saturn for the first time sparked an obsession that couldn't be contained.

March 2022

Cosmic Explorer is Born

To share our stargazing logs and astrophotography, the first version of the Cosmic Explorer blog was launched. We started translating complex astrophysics into simple stories.

November 2024

Live Data Integration

We leveled up. Partnering with open-source astronomy communities, we integrated live NASA and ESA APIs, allowing our users to track satellites and exoplanet discoveries in real-time.

Present Day (2026)

A Global Crew

Today, we are a thriving community of millions. With the James Webb Space Telescope sending breathtaking images, our mission to democratize the universe is stronger than ever.

The Engine

Powered by Stellar Data

Our platform is built on top of the world's most powerful open-source astronomical databases and live space agency APIs.

NASA Open APIs

We pull live data from NASA's Exoplanet Archive and the APOD (Astronomy Picture of the Day) database to keep our cosmos updated daily.

ESA Gaia Mission

Utilizing the European Space Agency's Gaia dataset, we accurately map star positions, distances, and movements in our interactive 3D viewers.

JWST Live Tracker

Connected directly to the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI) feeds, we provide real-time updates and high-res infrared imagery from James Webb.

Stellarium Engine

Our live stargazing maps are powered by modified open-source Stellarium Web engines, perfectly calculating celestial coordinates based on your location.
