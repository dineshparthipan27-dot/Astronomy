Frequently Asked Queries

We have intercepted and decrypted the most common transmissions from our explorers. Find your answers below.

How do I book a private stargazing tour?

You can book a private stargazing tour directly through our Secure Contact Form above. Select "Stargazing Tour Booking" as your mission objective, and our crew will get back to you with available dates and coordinates.

Can I rent telescope equipment for off-site use?

Yes, we offer premium Dobsonian and Refractor telescopes for rental. An advanced booking of at least 48 earth hours is required, along with a security deposit.

What happens if it's cloudy on the night of my tour?

Space weather is unpredictable! If cloud cover exceeds 70% or if conditions are unsafe, we offer a full refund or a free reschedule to the next available clear-sky night.

Do you offer internships or research partnerships?

Absolutely. We frequently collaborate with astrophysics students and universities. Send your research proposal or internship inquiry via the Comms Channel email provided in our datapad section.

How can I submit my astrophotography for a feature?

We love showcasing community captures! You can tag us on social media using our official coordinates (links below) or email high-resolution files directly to our press team.
