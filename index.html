Your monthly guide to meteor showers, eclipses, and locating constellations in the night sky.

Highlight

Constellation of the Month: Scorpius

Visible during the summer months, Scorpius is one of the brightest and most recognizable constellations. Look for Antares, the glowing red supergiant star sitting at the scorpion's heart.

Upcoming Sky Events (Aug 2026)

Aug 12 - 13

Perseid Meteor Shower Peak

One of the best meteor showers of the year, producing up to 60 meteors per hour. Best viewed after midnight from a dark location.

Aug 28

Full Moon (Supermoon)

The Moon will be located on the opposite side of the Earth as the Sun and its face will be fully illuminated. This one will appear slightly larger and brighter.

Sep 07

Saturn at Opposition

The ringed planet will be at its closest approach to Earth and its face will be fully illuminated by the Sun. The best time to photograph Saturn!
